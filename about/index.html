Background

I’m Terry, a full-stack developer based in San Diego, CA. I enjoy building thoughtful digital experiences across the stack—from responsive frontends with React and Next.js to backend services with Node.js, Express, and MongoDB.

I’m currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a focus on Web Design & Development at Full Sail University, where I’ve strengthened my skills in software engineering, user experience, and modern web technologies.

The diary side of this site captures consistency: documenting what I’m learning, what I’m building, and how my thinking evolves. The portfolio side highlights the technical and design decisions behind that work, including structure, accessibility, and maintainability.

I’m especially interested in projects where strong fundamentals matter: clean information architecture, responsive layouts, readable typography, and systems that can grow without becoming fragile.

How I approach work

Clarity first

I prioritize readable layouts, clear hierarchy, and interfaces that help people find what they need quickly.

Accessible by default

Semantic structure, keyboard support, focus states, and thoughtful contrast are part of the build—not afterthoughts.

Content with purpose

Whether it’s a diary entry or a project case study, I aim to make content easy to browse, scan, and revisit.

Iterative growth

I treat projects as living systems: ship a solid foundation, learn from it, and improve through consistent iteration.
